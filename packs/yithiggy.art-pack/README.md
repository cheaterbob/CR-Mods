# YiThiggy Art Pack

A collection of 22 graphical hairstyles, three animal-ear accessories, and two full-body outfits by YiThiggy. Every item is unlocked by default. Hairstyles appear under Hair; Cat Ears, Fox Ears, and Bear Ears appear under Hat. Furry Slime Suit and Furry BDSM Red appear under Upper and replace the complete hazmat-suit rig without retaining its mask artwork.

The source PNGs use the Core 64 by 64 player canvas. They are kept at their authored size without smoothing or rescaling.

Version 1.4.0 adds Furry BDSM Red, updates Furry Slime Suit to its opaque V3 artwork, and splits each animal-ear accessory into front and rear layers so it wraps around the player's head and hair correctly.

Version 1.3.0 added Long Green and the Furry Slime Suit. Blue Cat, Ginger, and Long Green use separate front and rear layers; only the rear section receives bounded movement, preserving the authored resting artwork while giving the longer hair secondary motion. The moving-hair support requires Captivity Reloaded 2.2.2 or newer.
