# YiThiggy Art Pack

A collection of 22 graphical hairstyles, three animal-ear accessories, and the Furry Slime Suit by YiThiggy. Every item is unlocked by default. Hairstyles appear under Hair; Cat Ears, Fox Ears, and Bear Ears appear under Hat and inherit the Core Bunny Ears behavior. The Furry Slime Suit appears under Upper and replaces the complete hazmat-suit rig without retaining its mask artwork.

The source PNGs use the Core 64 by 64 player canvas. They are kept at their authored size without smoothing or rescaling.

Version 1.3.0 adds Long Green and the Furry Slime Suit. Blue Cat, Ginger, and Long Green use separate front and rear layers; only the rear section receives bounded movement, preserving the authored resting artwork while giving the longer hair secondary motion. The moving-hair support requires Captivity Reloaded 2.2.2 or newer.
